## Full description

MedPay is a payment and outstanding-management app for medical stores, agencies and pharmacy businesses.

See the information that matters at a glance:

- Total outstanding and overdue invoices
- Today and monthly collections
- Paid, pending and overdue payment status
- Six-month collection trends
- Store-wise invoices and payment history
- Agency-wise monthly register with stable serial numbers
- Paid, unpaid, due and no-entry filters

Manage day-to-day work:

- Add and update medical stores
- Record invoices and payments
- Automatically calculate paid and remaining amounts
- View invoice and payment details
- Search and filter business records
- Export and restore MedPay backups

MedPay keeps every business in a separate workspace. Account deletion is available inside the app, and an external deletion-request page is provided for users who cannot sign in.

MedPay does not provide lending, banking, money transfer, accounting, tax, medical advice or payment-processing services. It records the business information entered by the user and helps calculate and display balances.
